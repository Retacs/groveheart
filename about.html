Government

How Groveheart is run

House Groveheart and King Gidein hold the crown. Day-to-day matters are handled by a Prime Minister, elected by the citizens every 45 days, and the cabinet they appoint.

Minister of Welfare

Eyestrainz

Citizen affairs: immigration, housing and integration, plus handing out jobs and tasks.

Minister of Development

Retrocitrus

Infrastructure and land use: maintaining buildings and railways and running new public works.

Minister of Economy

Gidein

Business and trade: regulating shops, new trade relations, farms and emerald production.

Elections every 45 days

Any active primary citizen can run for Prime Minister. Candidates declare for 72 hours, then citizens vote for 48 hours.

Magistrate's Court

The High Magistrate runs a fair court, investigates crimes and settles disputes between citizens.

The GroveGuard

Under the High Marshal, the GroveGuard protects the people and lands of Groveheart.

History

From a duchy to a kingdom

  1. Nov 2023

    Groveheart City is founded

    Gidein starts building a settlement in the Duchy of :3, then part of the Principality of Nalora.

  2. 3 Jan 2024

    A state of the Imperial Federation

    The Naloran Council unanimously approves Groveheart as its own state within the Imperial Federation.

  3. 15 Aug 2024

    The Great Western Bay

    Groveheart and neighbouring Withervale leave the Imperial Federation and form the Great Western Bay.

  4. Sep 2024

    Western Bay Conflict

    The Great Western Bay wins a short conflict against Thanis0077 and his mercenaries.

  5. 7 Nov 2024

    Full independence

    With the rest of the Bay inactive, Groveheart declares independence and starts to grow quickly.

  6. 11 Mar 2025

    Alcantara returns

    The Imperial Federation sells the Alcantara peninsula back to Groveheart.

  7. 6 Jun 2025

    The Meridiem Entente

    Groveheart, Salerno, the Imperial Federation and Skandic turn their defence pact into a global alliance.

  8. 13 Sep 2025

    Cabinet government

    Parliament is replaced by an elected Prime Minister who appoints a cabinet of ministers.

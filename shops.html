Rules for merchants

  1. Merchants post their shop in #businesses.
  2. Shop owners are notified in their post when stock runs low.
  3. After a notification, owners have 14 days to restock as best they can.
  4. Can't get a certain item? Remove that shop chest or put up a sign saying it's out of stock. (Relying on this too often can be penalised.)
  5. Not restocking or fixing the chest within 14 days earns a demerit. The Ministry of Economy can make exceptions.
  6. After the first demerit, each further demerit comes after another 7 days.
  7. On the third demerit, the Ministry of Economy closes the shop and evicts the merchant.
  8. Shops that aren't cleared out after eviction have their leftover contents seized. Getting items back is negotiable.

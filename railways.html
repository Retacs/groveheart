Getting around

Railways

Ten lines leave from the central station in Groveheart City. Hop in a minecart and you are anywhere in no time.

Central station

Everything starts here

All lines leave from the station in Groveheart City. It is also the best meeting point for new players.

-5914 67 5578

Destination railways

Three networks, many stops

These lines serve lots of stations. Set where you want to go with the /dest command before you ride.

The Bayway

Groveheart's own internal network, linking the towns and settlements of the kingdom.

-5920 67 5578

The Imperial Railway

A vast network connecting every Imperial Federation settlement and their allies.

-5914 67 5578

The Sophline

An international line from Brunsvick across the eastern side of the map.

-5908 67 5578
Direct lines

Straight to the neighbours

No /dest needed: these lines take you directly to one station. Their platforms are one level down.

Central station

Everything starts here

All networks leave from the central station in Groveheart City. It is also the best meeting point for new players.

-5914 67 5578

How it works

Two levels, lots of lines

The three big networks run on /dest: type the command for where you want to go, then get in a minecart and ride. On the lowest level, the Groverail platforms add direct lines to our neighbours. The book or sign at each platform tells you where it goes.

No station found. Check the spelling, or look at the books at the platforms.

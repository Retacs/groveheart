Getting around

Railways

Three rail networks leave from the central station in Groveheart City. One command, and a minecart takes you across the map.

Central station

Everything starts here

All networks leave from the central station in Groveheart City. It is also the best meeting point for new players.

-5914 67 5578

How it works

Three networks, one command

Every line at the station runs on /dest. Type the command for where you want to go, then get in a minecart and ride. The book at each platform lists all of its stations.

Rail districts

The big network across the map, split into districts. Name the district first, then the station, for example /dest east Avalon.

Ride/dest <district> <station>

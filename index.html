Our way

We speak for the trees

King Gidein is our very own Lorax, in looks and in mindset. Groveheart is built around the spruce woods, not in place of them, and every citizen helps keep it that way.

Law of the Woods

Every tree in Groveheart is protected. Building, digging or clearing anywhere outside your own home or shop needs permission from the King or one of his appointees, and that includes trees.
